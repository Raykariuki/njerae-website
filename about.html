HER STORY

WHO IS
NJERAE?

Njerae is a Kenyan-based singer, songwriter and producer whose music identifies with Afro-indie, soul and R&B.

She has attained her Bachelor's degree in Psychology and hopes to one day open a counselling centre where music will be used as a form of therapy.

She has equipped herself with skills in live music performance and music entrepreneurship as a graduate of the Perform Music Incubator.

THE JOURNEY

HER MUSIC

2022

UNINTENTIONAL

Njerae released her album Unintentional.

The album allowed fans to interpret it as a girl's handbook when going through a breakup, allowing more female listeners to relate deeply with her music.

The project also saw Njerae featured on a number of radio and television shows.

2023

OTD & VALHALLA

Taking a significant step toward modern R&B and contemporary Afro-pop sounds, Njerae released the singles OTD and Valhalla.

The releases opened new doors for her in the music business and continued to establish her evolving sound.

2024

#MWAKAWANJERAE

2024 marked the beginning of the #mwakawanjerae era.

Njerae released her EP Out With The Old, featuring collaborations with Watendawili, Lisa Odour, Mbithi and Maali.

She also signed with Universal Music East Africa, marking a new beginning in her musical journey.
